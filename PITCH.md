# ResumeX — 3-minute pitch (simple English)

## One sentence

**ResumeX helps first-time job seekers write a clear resume. It is free, it needs no account, and your data stays on your device.**

## Script (read it slowly, it is about 3 minutes)

### 0:00–0:25 — Problem

"Hello, my name is ___. Writing a first resume is hard.
Students do not know what to write.
They do not know if their resume is strong.
And many resume websites ask for money or an account before you can download."

### 0:25–0:50 — Solution

"ResumeX is a website for students and new graduates.
You fill in your information, and you see your resume change live.
It has 16 templates.
It also has a checklist that tells you exactly what to improve.
It is free. No account. Your data stays in your browser."

### 0:50–1:55 — Demo (show the screen)

Click **Explore with sample data**.

"This is a sample person. All the data is fictional."

1. Change the job title.
   "When I type, the resume changes right away."
2. Click **View all checks**.
   "Here are nine simple rules. For example: contact details, summary, skills, and numbers that show results."
3. Delete the summary.
   "The score goes down, and the website tells me what to fix."
4. Change the template.
   "My text stays the same. Only the design changes."
5. Click **Review & download**, then **Download PDF**.
   "And now I can save my resume as a PDF."

### 1:55–2:25 — Technology and honesty

"I built it with React, Vite, and Tailwind.
The score is not artificial intelligence. It uses simple rules that anyone can read in the code.
I do not promise that every company system will accept the resume.
The draft is saved only on this device, so it does not sync between devices."

### 2:25–3:00 — Impact and next step

"This project is made for students and first-time job seekers.
It is based on an open-source project by Rafivali Shaik.
I redesigned the website, added the readiness checklist, the sample data, and a new template.
I used AI tools to help me write the code. I chose the idea and I tested the result.
Next, I want to add Arabic and French, and save drafts to a file.
Thank you."

## Before recording

- Open the live link in a new tab (not localhost).
- Start with sample data. Do not start with an empty form.
- Practice 2 times. Stay under 3 minutes.
- Do not say: real users, recruiters tested it, AI score, or guaranteed success.

## Judge questions (short answers)

**What is new compared to the original?**
"A new design, a readable nine-rule checklist, sample data, and a new template."

**Why is the score not AI?**
"Simple rules are enough, and they are clear. No personal data is sent anywhere."

**What happens after October?**
"Arabic and French, saving drafts to a file, and matching a resume to a job description."
